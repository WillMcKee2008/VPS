Lines – Lines connect two points, guide where the viewer looks, how they feel, and determine the movement of a picture. 

Shape – A shape is an enclosed area with or without edges. Shapes define objects, and how they can be organic or geometric. 

Texture – Texture determines how a surface would feel if you were to touch it, such as a slimy, smooth, bumpy, or hairy. 
 
Volume – Volume is the sense of a 3D weight and presence of an object in a picture. Artists create volume on a flat area by shading, and perspective.
